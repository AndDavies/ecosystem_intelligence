## Media pitch

Subject: New public map makes Canadian defence capability easier to find

True North Map is opening for broader public use as an independent discovery layer for Canada's defence and dual-use ecosystem.

The platform brings together more than 250 reviewed organizations, more than 240 technologies and offerings, 30 released public needs and more than 380 cited public sources. It combines a national map, natural-language discovery, evidence-linked profiles, Canadian Defence Briefs, private Working Lists and human-vetted connection requests.

The project was created by veteran and Combat Systems Engineering Officer Andrew Davies after repeatedly seeing capable Canadian organizations remain difficult to discover across fragmented regional, program and industry networks.

True North Map does not present its assessments as procurement eligibility or government endorsement. Research can be assisted by software, but every public record and technology-to-need connection remains subject to human review.

## Reddit and Indie Hackers

Canada has strong defence and dual-use companies, but finding them by technology, region, evidence or collaboration need is harder than it should be.

I built True North Map as a free public discovery resource. It maps more than 250 reviewed organizations and more than 240 technologies or offerings, with a synchronized national map, evidence-linked profiles, natural-language discovery, released public needs, exports, reviewed submissions and private introduction requests.

The central design constraint is trust. Research tools can stage candidates, but a person reviews every public change. Missing information stays missing, and possible relevance is kept separate from source-backed fact.
