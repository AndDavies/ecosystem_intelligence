Subject: Help make Canadian defence capability easier to find

defence and dual-use ecosystem.

It helps people move from a Mission Area, technology, place or released Public
Need to Canadian organizations that may be relevant. Visitors can inspect the
supporting evidence, see where information remains incomplete and carry useful

## Media pitch

Subject: Independent platform maps Canadian defence capability to real missions and public needs

True North Map is opening for broader public use as an independent discovery
layer for Canada's defence and dual-use ecosystem.

The platform goes beyond a company directory. It connects reviewed Canadian
organizations and technologies to Mission Areas and released Public Needs, then
shows the evidence, evidence strength and visible gaps behind each record.
Visitors can explore a national map, ask a plain-language discovery question,
read Canadian Defence Briefs and build a private Working List.

Veteran and Combat Systems Engineering Officer Andrew Davies created the
project after repeatedly seeing capable Canadian organizations remain difficult
to discover across fragmented regional, program and industry networks.

The trust boundary is deliberate. Research software can help find and structure
candidates, but it cannot publish. Every public record and technology-to-need
assessment is reviewed by a person. The platform does not claim procurement
eligibility, government endorsement or access to classified demand.

## Reddit or Indie Hackers

Canada has capable defence and dual-use companies, but finding them by mission,
technology, region and evidence is harder than it should be.

I built True North Map as a free public discovery resource. It connects a
national organization map with reviewed technologies, Mission Areas, released
Public Needs and cited public evidence. The point is to help someone move from
what they are trying to solve to Canadian organizations worth exploring.

The central design constraint is trust. Software can help find and structure
research, but a person reviews every public record. Possible relevance is kept
separate from source-backed fact, and missing information stays visible.
