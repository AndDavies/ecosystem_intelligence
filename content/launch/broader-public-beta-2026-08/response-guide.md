# True North Map response guide

Use these answers in public comments, direct conversations, partner briefings
and media responses. Keep the answer proportionate to the question and link to
the relevant public page when more detail is useful.

## What is True North Map?

True North Map is an independent, evidence-led discovery platform for Canadian
defence and dual-use capability. It helps people find organizations and
technologies, see where they may fit, inspect the evidence and move into a
better-informed conversation.

## Is it just a company database?

No. Organization profiles are one layer. Reviewed technologies are also
connected to Mission Areas and released Public Needs. That lets a visitor start
with a real problem, find potentially relevant Canadian capability and see the
evidence and uncertainty behind the connection.

## What is a Mission Area?

A Mission Area is a practical use case or operating problem, such as underwater
ISR or resilient communications. It organizes reviewed capability
relationships for discovery. It is not a claim of procurement demand, customer
interest or government priority.

## What is a Public Need?

Public Needs is the collection of released, source-gated Demand Signals. Each
individual signal must point to a durable public source and show where the need
was stated. It is different from a Mission Area, which is a navigation and
assessment layer.

## Does a displayed match mean a company is eligible for a procurement?

No. A technology-to-need connection is a reviewed assessment of possible
relevance based on public evidence. It does not indicate endorsement,
procurement eligibility, customer interest or a formal opportunity.

## Is True North Map a government, military or industry-association directory?

No. True North Map is an independent project created and stewarded by Andrew
Davies. Public sources may include government, allied, program and company
material, but those organizations do not operate or endorse the platform.

## Where does the information come from?

The public record is built from durable sources such as official organization
pages, government and allied publications, program pages and reputable industry
sources with direct evidence. Public profiles link back to supporting sources.

## Does AI publish records or make the matches?

No. Software can help discover leads, structure candidates and suggest possible
connections. It cannot publish. A person reviews every public record and every
published assessment.

## What can Ask True North do?

Ask True North translates a plain-language need into a constrained search of
known, published records. It can summarize the evidence returned by that
search. It cannot invent an organization, add a fact or make a procurement
decision.

## What does evidence strength mean?

Evidence strength describes how well the available public record supports a
displayed statement. It is not a quality rating of the organization or its
technology.

## Why is information missing?

Coverage is still growing, and some details are not available from durable
public sources. True North Map shows gaps rather than filling them with
assumptions.

## How can an organization correct or claim its profile?

Use the claim or correction control on the profile. A submission enters a
private review workflow and cannot change the public record automatically.

## How do Working Lists work?

A signed-in user can save organizations in a private Working List and add the
status, rationale and next step needed for follow-up. A Working List is not
visible to other public users.

## How do introductions work?

A signed-in user can explain what they need and what they can offer. Andrew
reviews the request and may facilitate a relevant introduction. True North Map
does not expose private contact information or connect parties automatically.

## What are Defence Briefs?

Defence Briefs are reviewed, source-linked explainers about Canadian defence,
industry and capability questions. They separate durable public evidence from
editorial interpretation and visible limits.

## What is North Signal?

North Signal is the weekly True North Map briefing. It highlights newly mapped
capability, released Public Needs, reviewed connections and defence developments
worth following. Andrew reviews each issue before it is sent.

## What information should not be entered?

Do not enter classified, controlled, confidential, proprietary or personal
information. True North Map is designed for public evidence.

## How is visitor data handled?

Essential security and consent records operate by default. Optional analytics
load only after consent and are excluded from private routes. The Privacy page
explains data use, retention and user choices.

## Who is behind the project?

True North Map is created and stewarded by Andrew Davies, a veteran and Combat
Systems Engineering Officer. The goal is to make Canadian capability visible
and help more of the ecosystem move into useful conversations.

## What is the best way to evaluate it?

Try one real question from your work, inspect the sources behind the results and
decide whether it gets you closer to a useful conversation.
