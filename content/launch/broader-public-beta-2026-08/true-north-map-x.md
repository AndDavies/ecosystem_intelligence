## Operating boundary

- Publish manually, at most one or two useful posts each week during the first
  60 days. Do not use a scheduler, automatic cross-posting, paid promotion or
  paid verification.
- Keep public facts, reviewed assessments and unverified gaps distinct.
- Treat replies, DMs and social posts as feedback or discovery leads. Direct
  corrections to the contact path and never use social material as published
  evidence without durable corroboration.
