# Founder-led LinkedIn announcement

Canada is building more than most people can see.

Through my work in defence, marine technology and innovation, I kept running
into the same problem. Canada has capable people, organizations and technology,
but the picture is fragmented. It can be difficult to see what exists, where it
may fit, and who might be worth speaking with next.

I built True North Map to make Canadian capability visible.

It is an independent, evidence-led discovery platform for Canada's defence and
dual-use ecosystem. You can explore organizations and technologies by place or
Mission Area, follow released Public Needs, inspect the sources behind a record,
read Canadian Defence Briefs and carry useful findings into a private Working
List.

There is an intelligence layer behind the directory. Reviewed technology is
connected to Mission Areas and released Public Needs so a visitor can move from
a real problem to possible Canadian capability, then see the evidence and the
limits of that assessment.

True North Map is not a government or procurement directory. A connection does
not indicate endorsement, eligibility or customer interest. Research software
can help find and structure leads, but people review every public record and
published assessment.

The public beta is open, and the map will keep growing.
