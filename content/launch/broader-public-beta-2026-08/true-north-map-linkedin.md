# True North Map LinkedIn page copy

## About

True North Map is an independent, evidence-led discovery platform that helps
people find Canadian defence and dual-use capability, understand where it may
fit, and move into better-informed conversations.

Explore organizations, technologies, Mission Areas and released Public Needs.
Inspect reviewed public evidence, build a private Working List and follow North
Signal for the capabilities and developments worth watching.

Reviewed public evidence. Transparent gaps. Human review.

Created and stewarded by Andrew Davies.

Website: https://truenorthmap.ca

## Launch post

Make Canadian capability visible.

True North Map is open for broader public use. Start with a place, technology,
Mission Area or released Public Need. Follow the evidence to Canadian
organizations that may be relevant, then carry the right ones into your next
conversation.

The platform is independent and transparent about what the public record does
and does not support. Possible relevance is a reviewed assessment, not
procurement eligibility or endorsement.
