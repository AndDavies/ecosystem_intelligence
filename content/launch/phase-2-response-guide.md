# True North Map public response guide

## What is True North Map?

An independent, evidence-led discovery platform for Canadian defence and dual-use organizations, technologies and released public needs.

## Is it a government or procurement directory?

No. True North Map is independent. Inclusion, evidence strength and possible relevance do not indicate endorsement, procurement eligibility, customer interest or a formal opportunity.

## Where does the information come from?

Public company pages, government and allied sources, program pages and other durable public evidence. Public profiles link back to their supporting sources.

## Does AI publish the information?

No. Software can help discover leads, structure candidates and suggest possible connections. A person reviews every public record and every published technology-to-need assessment.

## What does evidence strength mean?

It describes the quality and depth of public support for the statement. It is not a rating of the organization or technology.

## Why is information missing?

Coverage is still growing and some facts are not available from durable public sources. True North Map shows gaps rather than filling them with assumptions.

## How can an organization correct its profile?

Use the claim or correction controls on the profile. Submitted information enters a private review workflow and cannot update the public record automatically.

## How do introductions work?

A signed-in user can explain what they need and what they can offer. Andrew reviews the request and may facilitate an introduction. True North Map does not expose private contact information or automatically connect parties.

## What information should not be entered?

Do not enter classified, controlled, confidential, proprietary or personal information. True North Map is designed for public evidence only.

## How is visitor data handled?

Essential security and consent records operate by default. Optional analytics load only after consent and are excluded from private routes. Raw search text and detailed workflow events have published retention limits and scheduled deletion.

## Who is responsible for the project?

True North Map is created and stewarded by Andrew Davies, a veteran and Combat Systems Engineering Officer. The project aims to make Canadian capability visible and help the ecosystem move into better-informed conversations.
