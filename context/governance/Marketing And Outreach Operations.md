# True North Map Marketing and Outreach Operations

Status: canonical manual operating contract
Owner: Andrew Davies
Last reviewed: 2026-08-19

## Purpose

This contract turns True North Map's published product into useful, authentic
distribution without widening research, publication, provider, privacy, or
editorial authority. Marketing helps the right people discover a relevant
organization, technology, Mission Area, Public Need, Signals edition, or North
Signal sample. It does not manufacture demand or turn a public-source
assessment into an endorsement.

The fixed message hierarchy is:

- Promise: **Make Canadian capability visible.**
- Headline: **Canada is building more than most people can see.**
- Category: **Canadian defence capability discovery.**
- Positioning: **True North Map helps people find Canadian defence and dual-use
  organizations and technologies, understand where they may fit, and decide who
  is worth speaking with next.**
- Journey: **See who can help. Understand why they matter. Start the right
  conversation.**
- Founder thesis: **The capability was here. The shared picture was not.**
- Campaign offer: **Bring one real question. See what Canada can do.**
- Trust: **Public sources cited. Facts and assessments kept separate. Human
  review.**

`Evidence-led` remains correct in methodology, source review, evidence-strength,
legal, research, and governance language. It is not the public product category
or the default opening line for a post.

Search Console, private visibility reports, public sources, profile freshness,
and aggregate product behaviour inform topic choice. They are discovery inputs,
not proof of customer intent.

## Andrew's founder voice

Andrew writes as an informed builder and connector, not as a government,
military, procurement, or industry-association spokesperson.

Use this sequence:

1. Start with one concrete Canadian capability or coordination question.
2. Explain what became visible and why it may matter.
3. Link to the exact public record a reader can inspect.
4. State any material limit close to the claim it qualifies.
5. Invite correction, additional public evidence, or the next useful
   conversation.

Prefer plain language, specific observations, and first-person curiosity. Avoid
generic news summaries, hype, unsupported superlatives, institutional voice,
and posts that merely repeat a Daily Signals headline.

## Approved post types

- **Company capability note:** one organization or technology, the role it may
  play, what the public record supports, and one question worth exploring.
- **Problem-to-capability path:** begin with a released Public Need or Mission
  Area and show two or three relevant discovery paths without ranking suppliers.
- **Map observation:** a regional, technical, or role-based pattern visible in
  published coverage, with the limits of that coverage stated.
- **Founder build note:** why a product or workflow was changed and what user
  decision it now makes easier.
- **What changed:** one development that changes what a Canadian team may want
  to inspect next, linked to a published Signals edition or exact profile.
- **Open question:** a precise capability or coordination question that invites
  public-source contributions without soliciting sensitive information.
- **Partner amplification:** a relevant partner's public program or event linked
  to the exact True North Map context, with roles and attribution clear.

The campaign line **Bring one real question. See what Canada can do.** may frame
an approved pilot post or event invitation. It is not permanent site copy.

## Factual-check outreach

Pre-publication factual checks are narrow editorial verification, not approval
requests, paid placement, profile ownership, or a promise to publish favourable
copy.

1. Select a public business contact whose role is relevant to the record.
2. Identify the exact facts or interpretation being checked and link the public
   sources already used.
3. Ask for corrections, current public sources, and material context by a clear
   date. Do not request confidential, controlled, proprietary, personal, or
   classified information.
4. State that True North Map retains independent editorial judgment and may
   publish the record with the response, without it, or not at all.
5. Treat the reply as a lead until it is resolved to an approved public source
   or a governed first-party submission.
6. Keep contact details and correspondence private. Never put them in behaviour
   events, public metadata, or a marketing list.

Outreach never creates procurement eligibility, customer interest, endorsement,
rank, preferred-supplier status, or automatic publication. Corrections and new
claims use the existing private review workflow.

## Cadence and approval

The default pilot cadence is two posts per week, not an automated content
calendar:

- one Andrew founder/capability post;
- one True North Map product-proof, map-path, or Signals-connected post.

Quality and relevance beat quota. A week may contain one or zero posts. Andrew
must approve final copy, link, image, attribution, timing, and channel before
every post, message, partner handoff, campaign, or outreach action. Research,
visibility, Daily Signals, and North Signal workflows may prepare private
recommendations or drafts only. They do not post, message, import contacts,
change providers, or send campaigns.

## Attribution contract

Use these stable values for the founder pilot:

```text
utm_source=linkedin|x|tnm_linkedin|approved_partner_slug
utm_medium=founder_social|company_social|earned_partner
utm_campaign=tnm_founder_pilot_v1
utm_content=<topic_slug>_<post_type>
```

Use lower-case ASCII slugs with underscores. `post_type` should match a durable
family such as `company_capability`, `problem_path`, `map_observation`,
`founder_build`, `what_changed`, `open_question`, or `partner_amplification`.
Do not encode a person's name, email address, social identity, message text, or
other personal data in UTMs, cohorts, sessions, or behaviour-event metadata.

The destination is the most exact useful public route. Use `/` only for the
overall product story, `/north-signal` for acquisition, and `/signals/[slug]`
for a specific edition. Do not route every post to a generic signup page.

## Measurement

The private Admin Insights scorecard reuses existing `pilot_events`, session
attribution, consent-safe North Signal events, GA/Vercel aggregates, and raw
retention rules. No new marketing database, CRM, public endpoint, provider, or
identity graph is authorized.

Review campaign/cohort, source/medium, content, destination route, and these
meaningful outcomes:

- result selected;
- organization or capability dossier opened;
- cited source opened;
- record saved to a Working List;
- feedback provided;
- profile contribution submitted;
- connection requested;
- North Signal form started and consent succeeded.

Raw governed events remain intact. Scorecards exclude `/dev/`, explicit QA,
staff, test, internal, and approved automation-test cohorts. Page views and
impressions are diagnostic. The primary outcomes are useful record exploration,
return visits, saved work, substantive replies, corrections, contributions,
connections, and consent-backed North Signal joins.

## Prohibited claims and practices

- No claim of procurement demand, eligibility, endorsement, customer interest,
  official status, supplier rank, market completeness, or classified insight.
- No fabricated subscriber, traffic, engagement, customer, funding, deployment,
  readiness, or performance proof.
- No scraped bulk outreach, purchased list, badge-scan consent, silent
  newsletter addition, contact import, or legacy-group reuse.
- No automated social posting, direct messages, partner messages, or campaign
  sends.
- No use of search impressions or profile views as proof that a named buyer is
  interested.
- No publication of private provider data, raw queries, correspondence,
  personal information, credentials, draft research, or candidate records.
- No promotional copy that hides evidence limits or presents a True North Map
  assessment as a source fact.

## Operating checklist

Before an external action:

1. Confirm the linked record is published, current enough for the claim, and
   opens without authentication.
2. Re-read the source-backed facts, assessment label, review date, and material
   limits.
3. Use the approved message hierarchy and exact UTM convention.
4. Verify image rights, crop, alt text, and source attribution when an image is
   used.
5. Check the final destination and mobile preview.
6. Obtain Andrew's explicit final approval for the specific external action.

After an approved post or outreach item, record only aggregate campaign context
and useful outcomes. Corrections return to private review; provider, publication,
campaign, and outreach authorities remain separate.
