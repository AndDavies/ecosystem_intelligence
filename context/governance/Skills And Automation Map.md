## Purpose

This is the single registry for True North Map workflows, internal skills, schedules, executable contracts, providers, write authority, and human gates. It records the installed operating surface without making private skills or credentials repository content.

Production Supabase remains the only canonical queue and corpus. Local artifacts, issue packets, visibility reports, staging exports, and automation completion messages are never proof of live or published state.

The installed project-local research stages are the canonical skills of record and supersede cached or globally installed variants. Every validated `qualified` lead proceeds automatically to candidate building, evidence mapping, deterministic stewardship, and private Admin Review in the same active run; it does not pause for source-lead approval. Human authority begins with candidate review and remains separate from Publish.

The deployed executable interoperability surface recognizes `organization_bundle_v3`, `organization_refresh_bundle_v2` and the separately governed `organization_canonical_repair_bundle_v1`. Ordinary production corpus campaigns retain the `tnm-research-pipeline/1.7.3` evidence and dossier requirements; pipeline 1.8.0 adds canonical repair without weakening them. The tracked prepare command automatically verifies an equal or newer compatible production pipeline before creating a run, and import rechecks the actual candidate kinds and schemas immediately before intake. Operators do not synchronize patch strings manually; deployment does not grant staging, acceptance, publication, or template-activation authority.

Review contract v4 / pipeline 1.8.0 adds manual `canonical-repair` for 1-25 exact organizations, a private exact snapshot and `organization_canonical_repair_bundle_v1`. Source discovery covers identity/lifecycle lanes; the builder and mapper bind six supported operations to atomic evidence; stewardship rechecks collisions, dependencies, successor state and snapshot parity. Signal refresh is explicitly excluded. Every repair remains individual Review followed by separate individual Publish. Intake remains fail-closed unless the live endpoint advertises the exact compatible contract.
