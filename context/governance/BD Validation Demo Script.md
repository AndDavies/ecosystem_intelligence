# BD Validation Demo Script

Date: 2026-04-25

## Purpose

Use this script to test whether `Ecosystem Intelligence` helps a BD or engagement user move from a mission question to a defensible shortlist faster than their current workflow.

This is not a sales demo script. It is a validation guide for a guided conversation.

## Recommended Scenario

Prompt the participant with:

> You need to identify the strongest near-term engagement targets for Arctic Domain Awareness. Use the app to explain who you would engage first, why, what evidence supports the recommendation, what remains uncertain, and what next step you would take.

## Route Path

1. Open `/app`.
2. Open the featured Arctic Domain Awareness demo path.
3. Review `/use-cases/arctic-domain-awareness`.
4. Open `/use-cases/arctic-domain-awareness/briefing`.
5. Add one or more targets to a shortlist.
6. Open `/shortlists`.
7. Open the saved shortlist and add status, owner, next step, due date, and rationale.

## Facilitator Talk Track

### 1. Start With The Mission

Show the Mission Brief and Policy Alignment first.

Say:

> This app starts from a mission problem, not a generic company search. The goal is to understand the operational context, the public policy anchor, and the decision the user needs to make.

Watch for:

- Does the participant understand the mission framing quickly?
- Do the policy anchors increase confidence or feel decorative?
- Are any terms unclear?

### 2. Explain The Recommendation Layer

Open the briefing page and focus on the top targets.

Say:

> These target reads are derived analysis. They are not source-backed facts by themselves. The app separates why-this reasoning, limitations, evidence posture, freshness, and suggested next step so the user can challenge the recommendation.

Watch for:

- Does the participant trust the separation between fact and derived analysis?
- Do the target cards answer enough without opening every profile?
- Is "why not others" useful or too generic?

### 3. Inspect Evidence And Gaps

Open at least one capability or company if the participant asks for proof, then return to the briefing.

Say:

> The app should not hide uncertainty. Gaps are part of the product because they tell a BD user where the map is thin and what needs more research before action.

Watch for:

- Does visible uncertainty increase trust?
- Are the evidence and freshness labels understandable?
- Are gaps actionable enough?

### 4. Save A Shortlist

Add one or more targets to a shortlist and open the saved list.

Say:

> The shortlist is a working memory layer, not a CRM. It preserves why a target was selected and what the next human validation step should be.

Watch for:

- Can the participant save a target without guidance?
- Are status values clear: Watch, Validate, Engage, Hold?
- Is owner / next step / rationale enough for follow-up?

### 5. Close With The Validation Question

Ask:

> Compared with your current workflow, would this help you make a faster or more defensible engagement recommendation? What would you still need before using it in a real BD conversation?

Capture:

- Current workflow replacement potential.
- Confidence in top targets.
- Trust in evidence and uncertainty.
- Missing data that blocks action.
- Which downstream workflow matters next.

## Success Criteria

- Participant can explain the top 3-5 targets without opening every profile.
- Participant can distinguish source-backed facts from derived analysis.
- Participant can save and revisit a shortlist with rationale.
- Participant identifies at least one concrete next step from the shortlist.
- Participant says the workflow is faster, clearer, or more defensible than their current process.

## Failure Signals

- Participant treats the app as just another database.
- Participant does not trust the recommendation logic.
- Participant needs to export everything and rebuild the answer manually.
- Participant cannot identify what to do next from the shortlist.
- Participant focuses only on missing breadth rather than recommendation quality.

## Product Guardrails During Validation

- Do not imply classified requirement access.
- Do not describe derived analysis as sourced fact.
- Do not hide weak evidence or stale data.
- Do not promise CRM, contact management, outbound sequencing, or procurement tracking in this phase.
- Do not expand broad real-world data ingestion until this workflow proves valuable.
